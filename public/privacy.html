Privacy

Last updated 1 September 2026

There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do here is sent anywhere or recorded about you. Your streak lives in your own browser and never leaves it.

What is stored, and where

The game keeps one entry in your browser's localStorage, under the key call-the-zone/v1. It stays on the device you played on. It is not transmitted, backed up, or readable by anyone else, and there is no server holding a copy.

StoredWhat it is
streak, maxStreak, played, lastPlayedYour daily streak and how many days you have finished.
historyPer day: the date, your score, and which of the five you got right.
bestRun, runsPlayed, pitchesSeenEndless-mode totals.
skinId, muted, introSeenChosen uniform, sound setting, and whether you have seen the how-to-play card.

That is the whole list. There is no name, no email, no device fingerprint, and no identifier of any kind — the save cannot be tied back to a person, because nothing in it identifies one.

Deleting it

Clearing site data for this domain in your browser erases the save completely and immediately. There is nothing else to delete and nobody to ask, because no copy exists anywhere else.

Other parties

  • Vercel hosts the site. Like any web host, its servers process the normal request information — IP address, browser user agent, which files were requested — in order to serve pages and guard against abuse. That is Vercel's processing, covered by their privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the two typefaces. Loading a font discloses your IP address to Google, as any third-party asset does. This is the only external request the site makes.

Everything else — the pitch data, the 3D models, the game itself — is served from this domain.

Sharing your score

The share button builds the result text locally and hands it to your device's own share sheet or clipboard. Nothing is uploaded; where it goes next is entirely your choice.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Since it collects nothing from anyone, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this ever changes — if analytics or accounts are added — this page will say so, with a new date at the top, before it takes effect.
