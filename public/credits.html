Everything this game is built out of

Pitch data

Pitch locations, velocities, strike-zone tops and bottoms, and the umpire's recorded call all come from public Statcast pitch tracking, published through Baseball Savant (MLB Advanced Media). Data is retrieved with pybaseball.

Independent project. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Major League Baseball, any club, or any player. Player names appear only as factual description of publicly reported pitches.

3D models

  • Player mannequin — the Universal Animation Library figure by Quaternius, released under CC0 1.0 (public domain). Its 43 animation clips are stripped out and unused — every pose in the game is solved from code — which takes it from 7.6 MB to 0.62 MB.
  • Bat — a 33-inch wooden bat from a third-party Baseball Bats asset pack, converted and re-textured for this project. Full attribution for this pack is being confirmed and will be listed here.

Everything else on the field — the stadium, the plate, the dirt, the caps, helmets and the catcher's mask — is generated from code, not modelled.
